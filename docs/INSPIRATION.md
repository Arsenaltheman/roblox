# Inspiration audit: what we steal from ~30 games (October 2026)

Four research passes (top Roblox simulators, Roblox social/co-op hits, the long-lived retention classics, and the best mobile free-to-play designs outside Roblox) produced about 60 mechanics. This is the ranked cut. We copy **mechanics and feel**, never names, art or UI skins.

Compliance lines we never cross (several of these games got demoted or sued for them): paid randomness without live numeric odds; rewards for likes/favourites/watching feeds; fake timers or "limited" offers; purchasable admin powers over the economy; opaque lotteries. Everything below is deterministic or odds-disclosed, and no item here lets a player edit the economy.

## Rejected (and why)

- Coin Master slot machine, Monopoly GO tile-flip heist sizes, Royale High fountain: gambling-shaped or opaque odds.
- Paid sticker packs, 24-hour "golden" trade windows tied to purchases: artificial urgency.
- Admin Panel / purchasable economy powers (Steal a Brainrot, 4,999–9,999 R$): the most criticised pay-to-win pattern; Steal a Brainrot itself walked luck purchases back.
- Tier-skip pass pricing (Marvel Snap Premium+), VIP tiers (AFK Arena): pay-to-progress pressure on kids.
- Rewarded media feeds: the exact thing that got Steal an Egg delisted.
- Steal only from banked cash (Coin Master): our critters-as-stakes loop is the genre's point; we keep harsh theft but with protection, revenge and clear rules.
