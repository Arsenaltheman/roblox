## 1. Competitors

### 1.1 Steal a Brainrot (SAB)
**Launch and owners**
- Launched May 16, 2025 by the Roblox group BRAZILIAN SPYDER ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/109983668079237)).
- Dev: SpyderSammy. Owner: Do Big Studios ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)).
- Lawsuits are filed by Spyder Games LLC / Speedy Simulator Gaming ([Bloomberg via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/03/25/robloxs-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators)).

**Core loop**
- **Servers:** 8 players per server. Bases start with one floor and 8 slots ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sim/one-of-robloxs-biggest-experiences-right-now-is-a-bizarre-italian-brainrot-character-stealing-simulator-and-lord-help-me-i-now-understand-why-its-so-popular/)). Later guides say ~10 slots, growing with rebirths **[conflict]**.
- **Conveyor:** spawns a brainrot every 2–4 s. You start with $100. Noobini Pizzanini costs $25 and earns $1/s, a 25 s payback ([rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/steal-a-brainrot/updates/steal-a-brainrot-beginners-guide-2026)).
- **Base lock:** auto-locked 30 s when you join. After that, a lock lasts 60 s + 10 s per rebirth, with laser bars ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/?p=6441)).
- **Stealing:** press E on a unit in an unlocked base. Carrying slows you and alerts the owner. Getting hit drops the unit back. Reaching home makes it yours ([Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=259152)).
- **Rebirths:**
  - R1 costs $1M plus two specific units, and gives +0.5× income and +10 s lock ([Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=274062)).
  - R2 costs $3M and unlocks the 2nd floor ([Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=274085)).
  - The 3rd floor comes at R10. Guides go past R16 ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/steal-brainrot-rebirth-16-guide)).
- **Gear by rebirth:** speed coil $750 → invisibility cloak (R4) → … → Quantum Cloner (R7, bypasses lasers) ([Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/steal-a-brainrot-coin-shop/)).

**Rarities, mutations, events**
- **Rarities:** Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic → Brainrot God → Secret → OG ([Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/all-brainrots-in-steal-a-brainrot/)).
- **Mutations:** Gold 1.25× … Rainbow 10× … Disco 12× (event-only). Rainbow appears ~1% of the time ([rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/steal-a-brainrot/updates/steal-a-brainrot-mutations-multipliers-2026)) **[unverified]**.
- **Event traits:** up to 6× ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/steal-brainrot-all-traits-multipliers)).
- **Events:** Saturday updates plus "Admin Abuse" (3 PM ET), and "Taco Tuesday" ([Designbeep](https://designbeep.com/2026/09/05/when-is-the-next-admin-abuse-in-steal-a-brainrot/)).
- **Rivalry stunt:** an "Admin Abuse War" against Grow a Garden on Aug 23, 2025 ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/poll-who-will-win-admin-abuse-war-grow-a-garden-vs-steal-a-brainrot/amp/)).

**Players and money**
- **Peak:** 25.8M players at once on Oct 11, 2025, the first game ever past 25M ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players)).
- **Now:** ~200K, down 99% ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)). It was #7 on Sept 27, 2026 ([rblxdb](https://rblxdb.com/charts/most-played)). Total visits: 73.8B (Rolimons).
- **Revenue:** ~$64M from May 2025 to Mar 2026 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-24/roblox-s-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators)).
- **Spin-offs:** a movie deal ([Story Kitchen](https://www.story-kitchen.com/news/story-kitchen-partnering-with-steal-a-brainrot-creators-on-feature-adaptation-of-roblox-game)) and toys ([License Global](https://www.licenseglobal.com/toys-games/phatmojo-secures-global-toy-rights-to-steal-a-brainrot-)).

**Why it went viral**
- A ready-made "Italian brainrot" meme cast from TikTok ([Buffget](https://buffget.com/news/steal-a-brainrot-the-full-origin-story-of-italian-brainrot-memes-from-tiktok-absurdity-to-viral-chaos-13azgyx)).
- Short-video material: tens of millions of views of steals and "children crying" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)).
- The appeal is "how you can steal from and screw over other players" ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/steal-a-brainrot-roblox-empire-review-popular-memes-1851787027)).

**Criticism**
- **Pay-to-win** (Polygon via Wikipedia): admin commands sold for Robux.
- **Kotaku:** "an ugly, strange game that invites you to steal from and betray your fellow gamers".
- **Cheating:** exploit auto-steal scripts, plus auto-joiner bots that hop to servers with Secret spawns ([Dex Notifier](https://dexnotifier.lovable.app/)).
- **Real-money trading:** eBay sales ([eBay](https://www.ebay.de/itm/286904236554)).
- **Real-world harm:** school bullying and fights in Japan ([Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/roblox-steal-a-brainrots-huge-popularity-among-japanese-kids-raises-concerns-over-school-violence-microtransactions-and-excessive-play-sessions/)).
- **Lawsuits against copycats** over UI, art and animations ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/brainrot-roblox-court/), [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-steal-a-brainrot-fortnite-stealing-brainrots-lawsuit/)).

## 2. Roblox platform rules (2025–2026)

### 2.1 Paid random items
- **Definition:** any random outcome bought with Robux or with Robux-buyable currency ([Roblox docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)). It covers four types:
  - capsules (eggs, wheels, chests);
  - enhancements;
  - combinations;
  - **probability modifiers** (luck boosts, pity systems, rate-ups).
- **Odds disclosure:**
  - every outcome as a %, summing to 100;
  - shown before purchase;
  - updated live when modifiers apply.
- **If `PolicyService.ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` is true,** use one of six alternatives:
  1. a free earnable path;
  2. a fixed order;
  3. direct purchase at expected value;
  4. hide;
  5. block;
  6. teleport away.

  Use PolicyService in a pcall ([PolicyService](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/PolicyService)).
- **Trading:** `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` gates trading of paid items.
- **2026 tightening:**
  - odds for every individual item, not just categories ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/clarifying-requirements-for-paid-random-items/4654622)) **[unverified]**;
  - pressure from South Korea's loot-box law ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319148/20260626/koreas-loot-box-rules-push-roblox-disclose-item-odds-worldwide.htm)).

### 2.2 Presenting products honestly
From the [Monetization guide](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization):
- No false "almost out of stock" or "short time only" claims.
- No inaccurate or auto-restarting timers.
- No item that is "always on sale".
- Avoid "pushy or urgent messages" to minors.
- Appointment timers and paid cooldown removal are discouraged.

Discovery and thumbnails:
- Thumbnails must not misrepresent gameplay ([Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)).
- Metadata implying monetary rewards is not recommended ([Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)).

### 2.3 Accounts, age and parental controls
- **Maturity labels:** Minimal and Mild games are eligible for **Roblox Kids** (5–8) and **Select** (9–15). Moderate games are for Select plus 16+ ([Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)).
- **Kids/Select listing requirements** ([Kids & Select](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select)):
  - a verified creator with 2FA;
  - **Roblox Plus/Premium for 2 consecutive months**;
  - 250 plays by highly engaged, age-checked players within 60 days.
- **Age checks** have been required for chat worldwide since Jan 2026 ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/01/roblox-age-checks-required-to-chat)).
- **No in-game Discord/X prompts** since about Feb 2026 ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/to-what-extent-can-you-mention-xtwitter-in-game-in-2026/4530110)) **[unverified]**.
- **Parental spending caps and alerts** ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/how-to-set-up-spending-limits-and-notifications-on-roblox)).

### 2.4 Ads, subscriptions, pricing
- **Rewarded video ads** ([doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-ads)):
  - the creator must be 13+ and ID-verified;
  - the game needs ≥ 2,000 monthly unique visitors;
  - rewards are fixed developer products worth 3–10 Robux, never random, and never a progress gate.
- **Subscriptions:** start at 49 R$ ([doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/subscriptions)).
- **Price optimization:** needs ≥ 60K transactions in 30 days, and prices must be read at runtime ([doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/price-optimization)).
- **Private servers:** changing the price cancels every active subscription ([doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/private-servers)).

## 3. Discovery algorithm and benchmarks

**What the algorithm rewards** ([Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery); [DevForum, Apr–Jun 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)):
- Most important:
  - play-through rate;
  - first-play bounce (players leaving within 60 s and 61–180 s);
  - play days at D1, D2–7 and D8–28;
  - playtime, **capped at 60 min per player per day**.
- Also important: days playing with friends, qualified sessions, spend days.
- The measurement window grew from 7 to 28 days.

**GameAnalytics 2026 report** (500+ games with ≥ 1M monthly users) [[GA](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)] **[unverified]**:

| Metric | Median | Top 10% | Top 1% |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 retention | 10.3% | 15.9% | 22.2% |
| D7 retention | 1.6% | — | 9.1% |
| D30 retention | 0.5% | — | 4.7% |
| Session length | 9.8 min | 14.7 min | 21.1 min |

**Mobile:** about 80% of Roblox users ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/80-of-roblox-users-are-on-mobile-contributing-46-of-robux-revenue/)) **[unverified year]**.

## 4. Ethics and legal pressure (brief)

**Lawsuits and complaints**
- At least 10 US state attorney-general lawsuits against Roblox since Aug 2025, plus a federal multi-district case ([ConsumerNotice](https://www.consumernotice.org/legal/roblox-lawsuit/)).
- Settlements in 2026 ([ClaimsJournal](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/04/22/337051.htm)).
- An FTC complaint by Fairplay/NCOSE (May 2026) targeting paid random items, hidden costs and scarcity marketing ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/20/exclusive-advocacy-groups-file-complaint-roblox-manipulative-design/)).

**Research findings**
- University of Sydney: misleading monetization in 14 of 15 top-earning games, with near-misses and fake urgency counted as deceptive ([report](https://ses.library.usyd.edu.au/handle/2123/35033)).

**Loot-box laws by country**
- Belgium: ban.
- Brazil: ban for minors (2026).
- Australia: rating rules.
- South Korea: odds disclosure plus damages.

## 5. Tooling and pipeline facts

**Code tools**
- Rojo 7.7.1 (Oct 2026) ([changelog](https://github.com/rojo-rbx/rojo/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md)).
- Lune 0.10.5 ([changelog](https://github.com/lune-org/lune/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)).
- luau-lsp 1.70.1, StyLua 2.5.2, selene 0.32.
- Knit is archived: use plain modules ([ARCHIVAL](https://github.com/Sleitnick/Knit/blob/main/ARCHIVAL.md)).
- ProfileStore for saves ([repo](https://github.com/MadStudioRoblox/ProfileStore)).
- Vide for UI ([repo](https://github.com/centau/vide)).
- frktest for headless tests ([repo](https://github.com/itsfrank/frktest)).

**Open Cloud**
- **Luau Execution:** runs Luau on a real Roblox server against a place version, with a 1–300 s timeout ([docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud/reference/features/luau-execution)).
- **Place Publishing:** doesn't update SurfaceAppearance, EditableMesh or unions ([guide](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud/guides/usage-place-publishing.md)).
- **Assets API:** handles FBX/glTF models, images and audio; audio is capped at 100/month if ID-verified ([guide](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud/guides/usage-assets.md)).
- **Configs:** live-tunable values ([guide](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/cloud/guides/configs.md)).

**Studio MCP**
- Built into Studio: execute_luau, playtest, screen_capture, generate_mesh and more ([doc](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/studio/mcp.md)).
- Only reachable from a Claude session on the PC that runs Studio.

**Meshes, VFX, UI and audio**
- **Mesh specs:** ≤ 20K triangles per mesh, ≤ 4 bone influences, FBX settings as specified ([specs](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/art/modeling/specifications.md)).
- **VFX:**
  - ParticleEmitter flipbooks up to 8×8 or custom; 100 particles/s per emitter on mobile; flipbooks disabled on low-memory phones ([particles](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/effects/particle-emitters.md)).
  - Highlight costs up to 1 ms on mobile ([highlighting](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/effects/highlighting.md)).
- **New UI features:** UIStroke sizing modes, UIShadow, per-corner UICorner, radial/conical gradients, StyleSheets ([appearance modifiers](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/ui/appearance-modifiers.md)).
- **Fonts:** LuckiestGuy, FredokaOne, Bangers, BuilderSans exist in `Enum.Font`; Gotham is deprecated ([Font enum](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/reference/engine/enums/Font.yaml)).
- **Audio:**
  - The new Audio API (AudioPlayer, Wire, AudioDeviceOutput, effects) replaces Sound ([audio objects](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/audio/objects.md)).
  - The Creator Store has 100K+ licensed tracks and SFX ([audio assets](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/audio/assets.md)).

**ElevenLabs**
- Paid plans include commercial rights ([terms.law summary](https://terms.law/ai-output-rights/elevenlabs/)) **[unverified]**.
- Eleven Music's self-serve terms exclude "Studio Games" ([Eleven Music terms](https://elevenlabs.io/eleven-music-model-specific-terms)). Get confirmation before using it for music.

**Free 3D assets**
- CC0 sources: Kenney, Quaternius, Poly Haven ([Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license)).
- Sketchfab is moving to Fab: check each asset's license.

**Figma MCP**
- Starter plan / View seat = 6 read calls per month.
- Writing outside drafts needs a Full seat.
